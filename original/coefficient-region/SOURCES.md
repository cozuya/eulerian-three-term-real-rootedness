## Brief search actually performed

Queries included Eulerian polynomials with “linear combinations,” “three
consecutive,” “real-rootedness,” “Turan,” and “middle coefficient.” The last
phrase led from a citation snippet on the Zhang–Kitaev stack-sorting paper's
page to Ma's actual preprint. The snippet was not attributed to the wrong paper.

The check also encountered Liu–Yan, *Zeros distribution of the reverse strong
Turan expressions of polynomials sequences*, DOI
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2022.102426, and Gao–Liu,
*Hurwitz Stability of Generalized Turan Expressions of Polynomial Sequences*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04150. No theorem from either is used. Only the latter
abstract was retrieved; attempts to retrieve its full text failed.
